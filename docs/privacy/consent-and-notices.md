# Consent and Privacy Notices
## P11 — Verified Alumni Mentorship Platform
**Version:** 1.0.0  

---

## 1. Student Privacy Notice & Onboarding Consent
Prior to accessing mentor discovery, students must explicitly acknowledge:
1. **Purpose of Data Collection**: Academic programme, career goals, and skills are collected strictly to compute mentorship recommendations and track career progress.
2. **Visibility**: Profile details and goals are shared only with verified alumni mentors upon submitting a mentorship request.
3. **Communication Boundaries**: All mentorship conversations and session links must remain within the P11 platform. Solicitation of mentors' personal email, phone numbers, or off-platform messaging is prohibited and monitored.
4. **Institutional Reporting**: Aggregated, non-identifiable engagement statistics are shared with the university Placement Office to improve institutional career services.

---

## 2. Alumni Mentor Privacy Guarantee & Consent
Prior to submitting verification, alumni must review and consent to:
1. **Zero Public Directory Leakage**: Direct contact coordinates (email, phone, home address) will **never** be displayed to students or search engines.
2. **Mentoring Capacity Control**: Mentors retain unilateral control over their maximum mentee load and may pause requests at any time without penalty.
3. **Verification Integrity**: Uploaded credentials will be used solely by institutional Alumni Relations Officers to verify graduation credentials and will be purged in accordance with institutional data retention schedules.
4. **Third-Party Integrations**: Connecting Google or Microsoft calendars authorizes read-only busy/free status and event scheduling for confirmed mentorship sessions; private personal event titles are never read or stored.
